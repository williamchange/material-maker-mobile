Touch Gestures
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

While not an exhuaustive list, this shows the most
common touch gestures available in Material Maker for Android.

In general, most desktop inputs can be mapped to
touch inputs in the following manner:

Destop Mapping
++++++++++++++
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| Tap                                                   | LMB Click                                          |
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| Double-Tap                                            | LMB Double Click                                   |
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| Long-Press                                            | RMB Click                                          |
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+

Graph Editor
++++++++++++
+------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| Long-Press                                           | RMB Click                                          |
+------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+

Graph Editor
++++++++++++
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| Two-Finger drag                                       | Pan                                                |
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| Two-Finger Pinch | Double-Tap + Drag                  | Zoom in/out                                        |
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| Long Tap                                              | Add node menu                                      |
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| One-Finger Drag                                       | Box selection                                      |
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| Double Tap                                            | Enters subgraph/nodegroup, or exit/go up a level   |
|                                                       | in subgraph hierarchy when double-tapping in an    |
|                                                       | empty area.                                        |
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| Two-Finger Tap                                        | Undo                                               |
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| Three-Finger Tap                                      | Redo                                               |
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+

Nodes
+++++
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| Long Tap                                              | Open expression editor (float parameter)           |
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+

Reference
+++++++++
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| Two-Finger drag                                       | Pan                                                |
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| Two-Finger Pinch | Double-Tap + Drag                  | Zoom in/out                                        |
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+

Preview 2D
++++++++++
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| One-Finger Drag                                       | Pan                                                |
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| Two-Finger Pinch | Double-Tap + Drag                  | Zoom in/out                                        |
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| Two-Finger Tap                                        | Reset view                                         |
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+

Preview 3D
++++++++++
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| One-Finger Drag                                       | Orbit                                              |
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| Two-Finger Pinch | Double-Tap + Drag                  | Zoom in/out                                        |
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+

Library
+++++++
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| One-Finger Drag | Two-Finger Drag                     | Scroll                                             |
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| One-Finger Drag                                       | Drag item from libary panel (at icon)              |
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| Two-Finger Pinch | Double-Tap + Drag                  | Zoom in/out                                        |
+-------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
